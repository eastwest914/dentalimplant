Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about seeing a dental implant periodontist, dental implant cost and teeth implants price.

What does a dental implant periodontist do?

A periodontist is a dentist who specializes in gum disease and the bone and gum tissue that support teeth. A dental implant periodontist uses that expertise to place implants, assess whether your gums and jawbone can support them, and treat problems such as gum disease or bone loss. During your consultation, we can explain who will be involved in your care and what to expect.

How much does a dental implant cost?

Dental implant cost depends on how many teeth you are replacing, whether you need bone grafting or other preparation, and the type of replacement tooth you choose. Because every mouth is different, we give you a written estimate after an exam and imaging rather than a one-size-fits-all number. Call 267-388-9469 to schedule a consultation.

What is the teeth implants price for a full arch or full mouth?

The teeth implants price for All-on-4 or full mouth dental implants is higher than for a single implant, but it replaces an entire arch or both arches. The final price depends on the number of implants, the materials used and any added procedures. We review all of this with you in advance and talk through available payment options.

What is All-on-4, and is it right for me?

All-on-4 uses four implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth. It can be a good option for patients missing most or all of their teeth, and it often avoids the need for extensive bone grafting. Whether it suits you depends on your bone density, gum health and goals, which we evaluate during your visit.

How long do dental implants last, and how do I care for them?

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, dental implants are designed to last many years, and the implant itself can often last a lifetime. Brush and floss daily, keep up with professional cleanings, and let us know right away about any bleeding, swelling or looseness around an implant.
